Written for curious dreamers

Chapter one01

At the edge of the woods

At the edge of the woods, where the day turned gold, a little lantern woke up beneath an old oak tree.

It had no map and no one to guide it. Still, its warm glow whispered, There is always somewhere new to go.

Chapter two02

A secret on the water

The lantern followed a trail of silver pebbles until it reached a quiet lake. The stars were hiding in its dark, glassy water.

With one brave step, it held its light over the shore. A family of sleeping ducks smiled in their dreams.

Chapter three03

Light travels farther

By morning, the little lantern had climbed the tallest hill. Below, the path looked less like a mystery and more like an invitation.

It learned that a light does not have to be large to help someone find their way.
