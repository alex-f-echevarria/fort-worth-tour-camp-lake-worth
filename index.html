Chapter one02

Once upon a time

At Camp Lake Worth, beneath the trees
Young LeRoy laughed and played
He chased the wind through fields of green
While daylight slowly strayed

Chapter two03

The Shriners

Beside the lake, a temple stood
With towers dark and tall
Within, the Shriners spoke in gloom
And shadows filled the hall

Chapter three04

LeRoy's Discovery

One night he found a hidden door
Beyond the torchlit flame
Then sparks leapt high and filled the room
And called him by his name

Behind him roared the temple flame
He ran beneath the moon
And though he fled the burning hall
His old life ended soon

Chapter four05

The Goat Man

Now when the sun sinks crimson-red
And daylight leaves the trees
A horned shape walks the lakeside path
Where none can feel at ease
