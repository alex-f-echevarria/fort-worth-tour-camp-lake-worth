Chapter one02

Once upon a time

There was a boy scout camp in the woods called Camp Lake Worth.

There was a camper named LeRoy Shuman. LeRoy was a curious young man who loved exploring the woods and playing camp games.

Chapter two03

The Shriners

Not so far from the camp, there was a temple overlooking the lake.

The temple was home to a group of foul men who called themselves the Ancient Arabic Order of the Nobles of the Mystic Shrine

Chapter three04

LeRoy's Discovery

One day, LeRoy stumbled into one of the Shriners' secret chambers. They were practicing their dark arts.

LeRoy interrupted them, but was not able to save himself. He fled into the woods, disfigured while the temple burned around him.

Chapter four05

The Goat Man

Some say that LeRoy still roams these woods today no longer the curious boy he once was.

But rather, the Goat Man, a creature of the night, haunting the forest paths.
